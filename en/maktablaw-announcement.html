ICFLP is building MaktabLaw: legal protection for education in Uzbekistan

The company’s new project brings an AI assistant on education law and ICFLP lawyers together in one system.

What MaktabLaw is

Artificial intelligence, professional legal expertise and real protection in a single ecosystem. ICFLP is building MaktabLaw, a large-scale LegalTech/EdTech project designed to set a new standard of legal security for Uzbekistan’s education sector.

From the first question to the AI assistant to professional help from lawyers: the law should be accessible, clear and effective for every participant in the educational process.

Why education needs this

Teachers, administrations, pupils, students and parents face questions every day that general information is not enough to resolve. Labour conflicts, inspections, disputed decisions, contractual relations and the protection of legitimate interests call for prompt, well-grounded support.

Two tracks

The first track, AI Legal Assistant, works with the regulatory framework of the Republic of Uzbekistan, including legislation on education, the status of teachers and labour relations. It produces answers with references to documents and helps check legal rules and prepare or analyse an application, complaint, motion or contract.

The second track, Human Legal Protection, is the ICFLP lawyers. They step in when the situation is complex: pre-trial settlement, support during inspections, on-site visits by specialists, and representation of interests in the manner prescribed by law and on agreed terms.

Artificial intelligence provides legal information and does not replace an individual consultation with a lawyer.

Who the project is for

For teachers, administrations of educational institutions, pupils, students and parents. What each group gets is described in detail on the project page.

Where the service will be available

The project architecture includes a web service, a Telegram WebApp and apps for iOS and Android. We will publish the links on the project page as soon as the service becomes available.

Educational institutions can already discuss cooperation with us: leave a request or call +998 (87) 300-10-20.
