MaktabLaw. Next-generation legal infrastructure for education

The future of education begins with confidence in your rights. We are building MaktabLaw, a single hybrid ecosystem that combines the capabilities of artificial intelligence with professional legal protection in the real world.

Mission

Changing the very principle of access to law in education

Our goal is not limited to building a chatbot. We aim to change the very principle of access to law in Uzbekistan’s educational environment.

The problem

Teachers, administrations, pupils, students and parents face questions every day that general information is not enough to resolve.

Labour conflicts, inspections, disputed decisions, contractual relations and the protection of legitimate interests call for prompt, well-grounded support. MaktabLaw is being created so that such support becomes part of everyday life in education.

How it works

The two tracks work as a single system

Artificial intelligence helps a person understand their legal situation, and a professional team helps them move from information to a practical solution. This is what fundamentally sets the MaktabLaw concept apart from an ordinary reference service.

AI Legal Assistant

Intelligent legal support 24/7

The platform’s digital core is a specialised AI assistant that works with the regulatory framework of the Republic of Uzbekistan, including legislation on education, the status of teachers and labour relations. Intelligent search across legal sources makes it possible to produce answers with references to documents, check legal rules, and prepare and analyse applications, complaints, motions, contracts and other materials.

The project architecture includes a library of legal materials and templates, requests to a specialist, request tracking, a web service, a Telegram WebApp and apps for iOS and Android. The goal of the digital track is to make legal information available regardless of time and region.

Human Legal Protection

From a digital answer to real protection

ICFLP stands behind the technology. The MaktabLaw hybrid model provides for bringing in specialists in the relevant field in complex situations, pre-trial settlement, support during inspections, legal assistance to educational institutions, on-site visits by specialists and representation of interests in the manner prescribed by law and on agreed terms.

Scenarios

How it is designed to work in practice

Three typical situations from the education sector and the path a request takes.

Teacher

Labour conflict

  1. QuestionA teacher is being transferred to other work and is not sure whether this is lawful.
  2. AssistantShows the relevant labour law provisions and helps prepare an application.
  3. LawyerIf the dispute is not resolved, an ICFLP specialist steps in.

Administration

Inspection at an institution

  1. QuestionA school has been scheduled for an inspection and needs to understand the rights and obligations of the parties.
  2. AssistantSelects regulatory documents and materials from the library.
  3. LawyerICFLP specialists support the school through the inspection and visit on site if needed.

Student or parent

Disputed decision

  1. QuestionA decision by an educational institution seems unfair.
  2. AssistantExplains your rights and helps draft an appeal or complaint.
  3. LawyerIf protection is needed, ICFLP represents your interests in the manner prescribed by law.

Scale

A project of national scale

MaktabLaw’s ambition goes beyond a single app. We aim to lay the foundation of a national legal infrastructure for education that can unite millions of participants in the educational process and thousands of organisations across the whole republic.

The scaling strategy includes developing partnerships with educational institutions, distribution through digital channels and exploring options for integration with eMaktab and HEMIS. We want legal support to be available directly in the digital environment where teachers, students and parents already handle their everyday tasks.

Questions and answers

What is MaktabLaw?

An ICFLP project at the intersection of legal and educational technology. It combines an AI assistant on Uzbek law with help from lawyers when a situation calls for real protection.

Does the AI assistant replace a lawyer’s consultation?

No. Artificial intelligence provides legal information and helps you understand the situation, but it does not replace an individual consultation. In complex cases ICFLP specialists step in.

How is MaktabLaw different from a reference service?

A reference service gives you the text of the law. In MaktabLaw, the ICFLP team stands behind the digital answer: from pre-trial settlement to representation of interests in the manner prescribed by law.

Who is the project for?

Teachers, administrations of educational institutions, pupils, students and parents.

Where will the service be available?

The project architecture includes a web service, a Telegram WebApp and apps for iOS and Android. The links will appear on this page as soon as the service becomes available.
