Terms and ways to reach us

  1. Consultation

    Tell us about your situation by phone, on WhatsApp or at our office in Tashkent. A lawyer will ask clarifying questions and tell you where to start.

  2. Analysis

    We study the documents, run a legal audit and give you an honest view of your chances before any contract is signed.

  3. Strategy

    We choose the route: negotiations, pre-trial procedure or court. We explain every step and tell you which documents we will need from you.

  4. Contract

    We fix the fee before work begins. The only extras are state fees and the lawyer’s travel outside Tashkent, if it is needed.

  5. Ongoing support

    We assign a specialist in the relevant field to your case. They represent you in courts and government bodies, report on every step and see the case through to the result.
