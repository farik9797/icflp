How we handle a case

Five stages from your first request to enforcement of the court decision.

Terms and ways to reach us

  1. Request

    Leave a request on the website, call or message us. You can also come straight to our office in Tashkent.

  2. Review and assessment

    We study the documents, run a legal audit and give you an honest view of your chances before any contract is signed.

  3. Contract

    We fix the fee before work begins. The only extras are state fees and the lawyer’s travel outside Tashkent, if it is needed.

  4. Handling the case

    A dedicated specialist is assigned to your case. They represent you in courts and before the authorities and report on every step.

  5. Enforcement

    A court decision is not money yet. We follow the enforcement proceedings until the funds actually reach your account.
