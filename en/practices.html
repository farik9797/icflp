Practice areas

Five areas of law for individuals, businesses and foreigners in Uzbekistan.

Cases we handle

We represent clients in courts, law enforcement and government bodies at every level.

Our core practice

Debt recovery

We recover debts between individuals and companies, from loans backed by a simple IOU to commercial disputes between large enterprises. We trace hidden assets and see court decisions through to enforcement.

Learn more

Individual rights

Civil, family, housing and inheritance disputes. We protect property and personal rights and represent clients in courts of general jurisdiction.

Learn more

Criminal defense

We defend clients at every stage of criminal proceedings, with a focus on fraud, financial misconduct and abuse of office.

Learn more

Business and corporate law

We support transactions, resolve commercial disputes and protect clients from dishonest partners. We work with sole traders and startups as well as industrial and oil and gas companies.

Learn more

International law

We protect foreign citizens, investors and expats in Uzbekistan without language or red-tape barriers, and help Uzbek citizens abroad.

Learn more
